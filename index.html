Questions before you sign up

When is Habiora out?

It's in the last stretch of development now. Everyone on the waitlist hears the moment it's on the App Store, and gets the Android date as soon as it's set.

Which platforms will it support?

iPhone first, Android shortly after. One waitlist covers both — tell us which you're on when you get the email.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing gets deleted. The tree simply doesn't grow that day, and the days behind it stay exactly where they were. If you need a longer break, rest the tree and pick it up later.

Can I track a habit I'm trying to quit?

Yes. Set the habit type to “Quit a habit” and you mark the days you stayed clear of it instead.

Do I have to pay?

No. Three trees, every species, every chart and every milestone are free. Pro removes the limit on how many habits can grow at once and pays for the app's development.

What do you do with my email?

It's used for the launch note and nothing else. No newsletter, no sharing it with anyone, and one click to unsubscribe.
